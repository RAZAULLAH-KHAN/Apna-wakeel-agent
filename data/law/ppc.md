# Pakistan Penal Code 1860 (PPC)

## Section 350: Criminal force
**Category: assault, general**
Whoever intentionally uses force to any person, without that person's consent, in order to the committing of any offence, or intending by the use of such force to cause, or knowing it to be likely that by the use of such force he will cause injury, fear or annoyance to the person to whom the force is used, is said to use "criminal force" to that other.
*Practical legal implication:* Slapping, shoving, grabbing, or pushing someone without consent is criminal force under Section 350 PPC.

## Section 351: Assault
**Category: assault, general**
Whoever makes any gesture, or any preparation intending or knowing it to be likely that such gesture or preparation will cause any person present to apprehend that he who makes that gesture or preparation is about to use criminal force to that person, is said to commit an "assault".

## Section 352: Punishment for assault or criminal force otherwise than on grave provocation
**Category: assault, general**
Whoever assaults or uses criminal force to any person otherwise than on grave and sudden provocation given by that person, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three months, or with fine which may extend to fifteen hundred rupees, or with both.
*Practical legal implication:* Publicly slapping someone on the street without lawful excuse is a criminal offense punishable under Section 352 PPC.

## Section 337 & 337-A: Causing Hurt (Shajjah)
**Category: assault, general**
Whoever causes hurt to the head or face of any person is said to cause Shajjah. If the slap, punch, or blow causes swelling, bruising, bleeding, or bone fracture, it escalates to specific categories of hurt (Shajjah-i-Khafifah or Shajjah-i-Mudihah), punishable with imprisonment and Daman/Arsh (financial compensation).
*Practical legal implication:* If someone slaps or attacks you and causes visible redness, swelling, cuts, or bleeding, go immediately to a government hospital casualty/medico-legal department (e.g. Civil Hospital, JPMC, Services Hospital) and obtain a Medico-Legal Certificate (MLC).

## Section 378 & 379: Theft
**Category: theft_robbery, general**
Whoever, intending to take dishonestly any movable property out of the possession of any person without that person's consent, moves that property in order to such taking, is said to commit theft, punishable with imprisonment up to three years, or with fine, or with both.

## Section 390 & 392: Robbery (Snatching / Street Crimes)
**Category: theft_robbery, general**
In all robbery there is either theft or extortion. Theft is robbery if, in order to the committing of the theft, or in committing the theft, or in carrying away or attempting to carry away property obtained by the theft, the offender, for that end, voluntarily causes or attempts to cause to any person death or hurt or wrongful restraint, or fear of instant death or of instant hurt, or of instant wrongful restraint.
Whoever commits robbery shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than three years nor more than ten years, and shall also be liable to fine; and if the robbery be committed on the highway the imprisonment may be extended to fourteen years.
*Practical legal implication:* Snatching a mobile phone, purse, or cash from a person on the road is legal Robbery under Section 392 PPC. It is a serious cognizable and non-bailable offense. Police are legally obligated under Section 154 CrPC to register an immediate FIR.

## Section 397: Robbery or dacoity, with attempt to cause death or grievous hurt (Armed Snatching at Gunpoint)
**Category: theft_robbery, general**
If, at the time of committing robbery or dacoity, the offender uses any deadly weapon (e.g. gun, pistol, knife), or causes grievous hurt to any person, or attempts to cause death or grievous hurt to any person, the imprisonment with which such offender shall be punished shall not be less than seven years.
*Practical legal implication:* Being held up at gunpoint or weapon-point for your mobile phone or wallet is armed robbery under Section 397 PPC. You have the right to an immediate FIR, mobile IMEI tracing through CPLC (Karachi) or Punjab Police / PTA DIRBS blocking.

## Section 161: Public servant taking gratification (Bribery)
**Category: business, police, general**
Whoever, being a public servant, accepts or attempts to obtain any gratification whatever other than legal remuneration, shall be punished with imprisonment up to three years, or fine, or both.

## Section 383 & 384: Extortion
**Category: business, cyber, general**
Whoever intentionally puts any person in fear of any injury to that person or property to induce them to deliver money or property commits extortion, punishable with imprisonment up to three years or fine.

## Section 415 & 420: Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property (Fraud / Scams)
**Category: fraud, general**
Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property to any person, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.

## Section 503 & 506: Criminal intimidation
**Category: police, women, cyber, assault, general**
Whoever threatens another with injury to their person, reputation, or property to cause alarm commits criminal intimidation, punishable with up to two years, and up to seven years if the threat is to cause death or grievous hurt or insult to a woman.

## Section 504: Intentional insult with intent to provoke breach of the peace
**Category: assault, general**
Whoever intentionally insults, and thereby gives provocation to any person, intending or knowing it to be likely that such provocation will cause him to break the public peace, or to commit any other offence, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both.

## Section 509: Word, gesture or act intended to insult the modesty of a woman
**Category: women, general**
Whoever intending to insult the modesty of any woman utters words, makes gestures, or intrudes upon privacy, shall be punished with imprisonment up to three years or fine up to 500,000 PKR.
